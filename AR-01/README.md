# AR-01 — Structural Adaptation

First research project of Ausmera Adaptive Research.

## Objective

Investigate dynamic neural network growth and evaluate whether
adding neurons during training can improve learning efficiency.

## Experimental archive

The available project archive contains:

- E04 and E05 experiment reports
- E05 source snapshot
- E06, E07, E08, E09 and E10 trial CSV files
- E06, E07, E08, E09 and E10 summary CSV files

## E10

E10 evaluates fixed network architectures and three adaptive
growth strategies on a 3-bit parity task.

The experiment uses 30 trials per architecture, with 6 training
samples and 2 validation samples.

The results show strong training performance for larger fixed
architectures, but poor validation performance across all
architectures.

These results do not establish successful generalization or
an advantage for adaptive growth.
